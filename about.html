WHO WE ARE

Built on operating experience, not advisory theory.

DeXM Management brings more than 20 years of international experience across the U.S., Europe, the Middle East, and the CIS in restructurings, liquidity stabilization, EBITDA improvement, and exit readiness — across the metals, construction materials, extrusion, millwork, automotive, and food manufacturing sectors.

We are engaged to address operational underperformance, liquidity pressure, and margin erosion — stepping in as interim leadership or as an embedded operating partner alongside PE sponsors, owners and management teams of manufacturing companies to stabilize cash, rebuild EBITDA, and restore and create enterprise value in industrial and manufacturing companies.

Track record includes a 235% EBITDA improvement, P&L responsibility of up to $520M, leadership of organizations with up to 1,000 employees, restructuring of $60M in debt, and a successful exit generating a >2x gross MOIC and a >19% five-year gross IRR.

DeXM partners with Boards, Investment Committees, lenders, and management teams to protect enterprise value and improve exit outcomes — from the first 30 days of a liquidity crisis through the discipline that sustains performance long after.
